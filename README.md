## 🎯 项目初衷 | Motivation

在本科课程学习过程中，我整理了一些个人学习复习资料，希望通过开源的方式与更多同学分享。

这些资料主要围绕课程核心知识、重要概念和考试重点展开，尽可能减少不必要的内容，帮助大家建立清晰的知识框架。

During my undergraduate studies, I compiled personal notes and review materials for several courses.

By sharing these materials, I hope to make course revision more accessible and efficient for fellow students.

The notes focus on essential concepts, key topics, and exam preparation, with an emphasis on clarity and structure.
